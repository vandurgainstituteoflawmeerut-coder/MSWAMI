About the Portal

Empowering legal education through technology.

MSWAMI is the official web presence of Vandurga Institute of Law, Meerut. This platform aims to provide easy access to institutional information, resources, and digital services for students, faculty members, and visitors.

Built with a focus on clarity, accessibility, and modern design, the portal serves as a central hub for announcements, academic support, and community engagement.
